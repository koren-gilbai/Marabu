# Reading Leo Strauss

A self-contained, interactive tutorial on Leo Strauss in fourteen sessions: the theologico-political predicament, opinion and the good, the discovery of nature, the philosopher and the city, the art of writing, ancients and moderns, the three waves of modernity, historicism and the fact/value distinction, Jerusalem and Athens, zetetic philosophy and the Kojève debate, liberal education, Strauss among the Weimar émigrés, the quarrels about Strauss, and a reader's protocol with a self-check quiz.

Open `index.html` in a browser. The page has no build step; it loads only Google Fonts.

Quotations from Strauss were checked word for word against the editions cited in each caption. The texts in Figures 0 and 5 were written for the lesson and are not by Strauss.
